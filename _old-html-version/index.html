How bulk ordering works

From quote to delivery, the whole process is handled on WhatsApp or online.

  1. Choose your products

    Browse the catalogue or tell us what your event or shop needs.

  2. Get your price

    Pay the listed bulk price online, or request a quote for larger or custom orders.

  3. Confirm and pay

    Pay securely by card, transfer or USSD. You get a receipt immediately.

  4. Receive your order

    We pack and waybill to your state, or you pick up from our Lagos Island store.

What is the minimum order for wholesale prices?

Wholesale prices start from 50 pieces per product. You can still buy single pieces at retail prices.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. We deliver to all states by waybill. The delivery cost is added to your quote before you pay.

How do I pay?

Pay online by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack, or by bank transfer after confirming your order on WhatsApp.
